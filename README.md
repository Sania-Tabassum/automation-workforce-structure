# Automation and Its Impact on Workforce Structure

## About the Project
MBA academic project conducted on Jindal SAW Ltd.

## Objective
To understand how automation influences workforce structure, employee roles, and operational efficiency.

## Work Done
- Conducted structured research and information collection
- Studied changes in employee roles due to automation
- Examined the impact of automation on workforce structure
- Analyzed its relationship with operational efficiency and productivity
- Prepared and presented the final project report

## Key Learning
Developed an understanding of how technological changes can affect business processes, workforce requirements, and organizational efficiency.

## Skills Demonstrated
Research | Data Analysis | Business Analysis | Report Preparation | Presentation
